Faith & Leadership

My Professional Journey

My journey through BYU–Pathway Worldwide and Ensign College has helped me grow academically, professionally, and personally. As I study Information Technology, I strive to develop the skills needed to become a responsible systems administrator who creates secure and reliable technology solutions.

Guiding Principles

  • Integrity — Using technology responsibly and ethically.
  • Service — Applying my technical skills to help others.
  • Continuous Learning — Developing knowledge and improving throughout my professional journey.

Leadership Reflection

I believe effective leadership requires humility, accountability, and a willingness to serve. In the technology field, leaders must make ethical decisions, protect information, and create solutions that benefit people and organizations.

Ethical Technology Commitment

As a future IT professional, I am committed to protecting user privacy, maintaining secure systems, and using technology in ways that promote trust and positive impact.

Personal Agency and Spiritual Growth

During this academic block, I experienced one of the most challenging periods of my educational journey. About a week before classes began, my community experienced a prolonged power outage. While searching for alternative ways to continue my studies, my mobile phone was stolen. The phone was an essential part of my learning because it enabled me to access course materials, participate in online discussions, communicate with instructors and classmates, and connect my computer to the internet whenever needed.

Losing my phone created significant academic, financial, and emotional challenges. I spent additional money traveling to locations with reliable internet access to continue my studies and recover important information. Although I was able to restore some of my data, other valuable information could not be recovered. Replacing the phone also required me to purchase another device through an installment plan, creating additional financial pressure. These experiences caused me to fall behind in my studies, but they also strengthened my determination to continue moving forward.

Doctrine and Covenants 58:26–28

"Men should be anxiously engaged in a good cause, and do many things of their own free will, and bring to pass much righteousness."

This scripture reminded me that while I could not control every circumstance, I could choose how I responded. Through prayer, faith in Jesus Christ, and the guidance of the Holy Spirit, I found strength to persevere. Rather than giving up, I remained committed to my education and continued developing my knowledge and skills despite the obstacles before me.

Lessons Learned

  • Faith in Jesus Christ provides strength during adversity.
  • Personal agency means choosing faith and perseverance over discouragement.
  • Preparation and stewardship are essential for protecting important digital information.
  • Integrity and resilience are qualities that strengthen both spiritual and professional growth.
  • Every challenge can become an opportunity to learn, grow, and serve others.

As I continue preparing for a career as a Systems Administrator, this experience has strengthened my commitment to integrity, accountability, resilience, and lifelong learning. I trust that the Lord will continue to guide me as I develop both spiritually and professionally.
