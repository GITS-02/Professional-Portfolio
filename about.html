About Me

My name is Festus Onojakaroma, and I am an Information Technology student through BYU–Pathway Worldwide and Ensign College. My professional goal is to become a Systems Administrator with a strong foundation in networking, cybersecurity, server administration, cloud technologies, databases, and IT infrastructure.

Throughout my studies, I have developed practical experience through projects involving SQL Server, Transact-SQL, Python, SQLite, APIs, Flask, Git and GitHub, HTML and CSS, networking, cloud environments, Windows administration, and technical troubleshooting.

My academic experiences have taught me to approach technical problems systematically. I have learned to identify a problem, investigate its cause, test possible solutions, verify the result, and document what I learned. This approach continues to shape how I learn and how I approach technical challenges.

My Technical Development

My development in Information Technology has expanded from learning individual technologies to understanding how different areas of IT work together. Database projects have strengthened my analytical thinking, programming projects have developed my problem-solving abilities, and networking and infrastructure activities have helped me understand how systems communicate and operate within larger environments.

I am particularly interested in systems administration because it combines troubleshooting, networking, security, user support, infrastructure management, and continuous learning. My long-term goal is to continue developing these skills while building professional experience in systems administration, networking, and cybersecurity.
