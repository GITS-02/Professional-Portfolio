# Reflection on My Development Journey

Throughout my academic journey at BYU–Pathway Worldwide and Ensign College, I have come to understand that growth is not only measured by academic success but also by how we respond to unexpected challenges. This semester presented one of the most difficult experiences of my educational journey. About a week before the start of the academic block, my community experienced a prolonged power outage. While searching for an alternative way to continue my studies, my mobile phone was stolen.

My mobile phone was an essential part of my learning. It served as a connection between my computer and the internet, stored important course materials, enabled communication with instructors and classmates, and allowed me to participate in online discussions and classes whenever I was away from my computer. Losing it created significant setbacks that affected both my academic progress and personal life.

The incident forced me to spend additional money traveling to places where I could access reliable internet services and recover some of my academic resources. Unfortunately, not all my important information could be restored. To replace the phone, I had to obtain a high-interest installment loan, which created additional financial pressure. These circumstances caused me to fall behind in my studies and affected my confidence.

Despite these challenges, I chose not to give up. Instead, I turned to the Lord in prayer and sought the companionship of the Holy Spirit for strength, wisdom, and encouragement. Through faith in Jesus Christ, I found renewed determination to continue learning and working toward my goals. Although I am still recovering from the impact of these events, I have learned that setbacks do not determine my future. My response to those setbacks does.

---

# Lessons I Learned

This experience taught me several valuable lessons that will continue to guide both my spiritual and professional life.

First, I learned that resilience is developed through adversity. Although the loss of my mobile phone and the prolonged power outage disrupted my studies and created financial and emotional challenges, I discovered that perseverance and determination enabled me to continue progressing toward my goals.

Second, I gained a deeper appreciation for preparation and stewardship. As an aspiring Systems Administrator, I now better understand the importance of protecting digital information through regular backups, secure authentication, and contingency planning. This experience reminded me that technology is valuable, but the knowledge and character we develop are even more important.

Third, I learned that exercising faith and personal agency go hand in hand. While I could not control the events that happened, I could choose how I responded. Through prayer, trust in the Lord, and consistent effort, I found the strength to continue my education despite the obstacles before me.

Finally, this experience strengthened my desire to become an ethical and dependable Information Technology professional. I want to use my knowledge and skills to help individuals and organizations build secure, reliable, and resilient technology systems while serving others with integrity, compassion, and excellence.

# Connection to Doctrine and Covenants 58:26–28

Doctrine and Covenants 58:26–28 teaches that we should not wait to be compelled in all things but should be "anxiously engaged in a good cause" and use our agency to bring about much righteousness. This scripture has become especially meaningful to me during this academic block. Although I could not prevent the power outage or the theft of my mobile phone, I realized that I still had the ability to choose how I would respond to those circumstances.

Rather than allowing disappointment and frustration to stop my progress, I decided to continue pursuing my education by seeking alternative ways to access course materials, communicating with my instructors whenever possible, and making every effort to stay engaged with my coursework. Although I experienced setbacks and continue to recover from the financial and academic impact, I have learned that exercising my agency means taking responsibility for my actions while trusting the Lord to strengthen me through challenges.

This experience has also taught me that the Lord often works through our efforts rather than removing every obstacle from our path. As I prayed for guidance and relied on the Holy Spirit, I found greater peace, renewed hope, and the determination to keep moving forward. These experiences have strengthened both my testimony of Jesus Christ and my commitment to becoming a trustworthy Information Technology professional who demonstrates integrity, resilience, and faithful service.
